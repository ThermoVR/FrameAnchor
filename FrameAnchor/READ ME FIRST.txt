FrameAnchor
===========
Keeps your Steam Frame + Vive tracker playspace locked to your real room, so the
floor and playspace stop jumping around mid-session.

Experimental: it has been tested in simulation, not yet on a real Steam Frame.


HOW TO USE
----------
1. Extract the whole ZIP first (right-click it > Extract All). Don't run it from
   inside the ZIP.

2. Double-click "Start FrameAnchor".
   - If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click
     "More info" and then "Run anyway".
   - The first start needs internet and takes about a minute. Later starts
     are instant and work offline.
   - A black window and the FrameAnchor window both open. Leave both open.

3. Start SteamVR, put on the headset, turn on your trackers, and let Space
   Calibrator line everything up like you normally do. Make sure your floor is
   right and your feet are where they should be.

4. Click the big blue button: "My floor and trackers look right - LOCK IT".
   The top of the window turns green and says "Locked".

5. Play. Leave FrameAnchor open. When the playspace jumps, it puts it back
   within a couple of seconds and writes a line in its log.

Next time, just start FrameAnchor before or after SteamVR. It remembers the
lock, so you don't have to click anything.


RECENTERING OR CHANGING YOUR FLOOR ON PURPOSE
---------------------------------------------
FrameAnchor puts things back within a couple of seconds - including changes you
make on purpose. So do it in this order:

   1. Click "Pause"
   2. Recenter / fix your floor / move a base station / redo room setup
   3. When everything looks right, click the big button. That saves the new
      lock and switches FrameAnchor back on by itself.


OVR ADVANCED SETTINGS PLAYSPACE MOVER
-------------------------------------
You can use OVR Advanced Settings' playspace mover (dragging the playspace with
a controller button) and its turning. FrameAnchor keeps your move instead of
undoing it: the top of the window turns blue ("Playspace being moved") while
you drag, and once you let go it keeps the new position and goes back to
watching for jumps.

Good to know:
- After FrameAnchor has fixed a jump, OVR Advanced Settings sometimes "forgets"
  that fix when you start your next drag, so the floor can jump back while you
  drag. FrameAnchor puts it right about 2 seconds after you let go.
- A reset bind / "Reset" button that puts the playspace back to its 0,0 spot
  is kept too, as long as FrameAnchor was already running when the playspace
  was last at that spot (normally true if you start FrameAnchor at the start of
  your session). If a reset ever gets undone anyway: click Pause, press reset,
  then click the big button - after that, resets work.
- Other moves that happen in one instant are treated as jumps and undone,
  except snap turns.
- SteamVR's own "Reset view / recenter" still counts as a jump: click Pause
  first, recenter, then click the big button (see above).
- If jumps stop being fixed after this update, untick "Keep my OVR Advanced
  Settings playspace moves" in the FrameAnchor window and send the log to
  whoever gave you FrameAnchor.


IF IT ASKS "PLAYSPACE MOVED SINCE YOUR LOCK"
--------------------------------------------
When you start a new session and the playspace is far away from the lock you
saved last time, FrameAnchor asks first instead of moving it:
- You changed the floor / room setup on purpose: click the big button.
- You didn't: click the orange "Put it back to my saved lock" button.


WHAT THE COLOURS MEAN
---------------------
Green   Locked and watching. All good.
Yellow  A jump was detected, or the headset lost tracking. It sorts itself out.
Orange  It needs you. Read the line under the coloured bar.
Blue    Connected, but you haven't locked yet.
Grey    Waiting for SteamVR, or paused.


IF SOMETHING SEEMS OFF
----------------------
- After you click the big button, the log shows how high it thinks each base
  station is. If that's nowhere near how high your real base stations are
  above the floor, your floor is wrong: click Pause, fix the floor, then lock
  again.
- It made things worse: click "Undo fixes". That puts the playspace back to
  exactly how it was when you clicked the big button, and pauses FrameAnchor.
  To only watch without changing anything, tick "Test mode" and click Resume.
- It says "Taking a break": another program keeps moving the playspace in
  sudden steps (another playspace tool?). Close it.
- "Base stations don't match the lock" for more than a minute: a base station
  was moved, or SteamVR adjusted where it thinks one is. FrameAnchor changes
  nothing while this shows. Get everything looking right and lock again.
- To start over: click "Remove lock".
- Windows says it blocked python.exe ("Smart App Control"): FrameAnchor can't
  run on this PC as it is. Don't change security settings - send a screenshot
  to whoever gave you FrameAnchor.
- It says "FrameAnchor is already running": it's already open. Look for its
  window in the taskbar. Only one copy can run at a time.

Don't use these at the same time as FrameAnchor:
- QuestLHSync or OpenVR-SpaceSync

A log is saved at:  %APPDATA%\FrameAnchor\frame_anchor_log.txt
Send that file if you need help.


WHAT IT CHANGES, AND HOW TO REMOVE IT
-------------------------------------
FrameAnchor fixes jumps by changing SteamVR's saved playspace position (the
same setting room setup changes). Those changes stay after you close
FrameAnchor - that's what keeps your floor right.

- Undo inside the app: "Undo fixes" (see above).
- Before its very first change, FrameAnchor saves a copy of SteamVR's
  playspace file here:
     %APPDATA%\FrameAnchor\backup\chaperone_info (before FrameAnchor).vrchap
  Last resort, to go back to exactly how things were before FrameAnchor:
     1. Close SteamVR completely.
     2. Copy that file into your Steam "config" folder (usually
        C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\config), rename it to
        chaperone_info.vrchap, and replace the existing file.
  (Or simply redo room setup.)

To uninstall: delete this folder, and the %APPDATA%\FrameAnchor folder
(paste %APPDATA% into the File Explorer address bar to find it). Nothing is
installed into SteamVR itself.
