FrameAnchor
===========
Keeps your Steam Frame + Vive tracker playspace locked to your real room, so the
floor and playspace stop jumping around mid-session.

Experimental: it has been tested in simulation, not yet on a real Steam Frame.


HOW TO USE
----------
1. Extract the whole ZIP first (right-click it > Extract All). Don't run it from
   inside the ZIP.

2. Double-click "Start FrameAnchor".
   - If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box, click
     "More info" and then "Run anyway".
   - The first start needs internet and takes about a minute. Later starts
     are instant.
   - A black window and the FrameAnchor window both open. Leave both open.

3. Start SteamVR, put on the headset, turn on your trackers, and let Space
   Calibrator line everything up like you normally do. Make sure your floor is
   right and your feet are where they should be.

4. Click the big blue button: "My floor and trackers look right - LOCK IT".
   The top of the window turns green and says "Locked".

5. Play. Leave FrameAnchor open. When the playspace jumps, it puts it back
   within a couple of seconds and writes a line in its log.

Next time, just start FrameAnchor before or after SteamVR. It remembers the
lock, so you don't have to click anything.


WHEN TO CLICK THE BIG BUTTON AGAIN
----------------------------------
- After you recenter or change your floor on purpose (otherwise FrameAnchor
  will undo it)
- After moving a base station
- After redoing the Steam Frame's room setup
Always get things looking right FIRST, then click it.


WHAT THE COLOURS MEAN
---------------------
Green   Locked and watching. All good.
Yellow  A jump was detected, or the headset lost tracking. It sorts itself out.
Orange  It needs you. Read the line under the coloured bar.
Blue    Connected, but you haven't locked yet.
Grey    Waiting for SteamVR, or paused.


IF SOMETHING SEEMS OFF
----------------------
- After you click the big button, the log shows how high it thinks each base
  station is. If that's nowhere near how high your real base stations are
  above the floor, your floor is wrong: fix the floor, then lock again.
- It made things worse: click "Pause". Then tick "Test mode" and click Resume.
  In test mode it only writes down what it would do, without changing anything.
- It says "Taking a break": another program keeps moving the playspace. Turn
  off space drag / playspace mover in OVR Advanced Settings.
- "Base stations don't match the lock" for more than a minute: get everything
  looking right and lock again.
- To start over: click "Remove lock".

Don't use these at the same time as FrameAnchor:
- OVR Advanced Settings space drag / playspace mover
- QuestLHSync or OpenVR-SpaceSync

A log is saved at:  %APPDATA%\FrameAnchor\frame_anchor_log.txt
Send that file if you need help.

To uninstall, delete this folder. Nothing is installed into SteamVR.
